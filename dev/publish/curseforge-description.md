# The Shooting Star Addition

An **unofficial**, fan-made addon for **[The Shooting Star \[Demo\]](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/the-shooting-star-demo)** by rimuru\_dev. It adds three skills to the Stellar Remote: **SA-01 · Halley**, a comet called down onto your crosshair, **SA-02 · Luna**, which brings the moon itself down, and **SA-00 · Stellar Convergence**, which fires every weapon at once.

## ⚠️ Compatibility — please read

Version **1.7.0** works with **The Shooting Star \[Demo\] 1.5.2** and needs **RimuLib 1.2.2** or newer (The Shooting Star needs it too). On The Shooting Star 1.5.0, keep version 1.6.0 of this addon; on 1.4.x, version 1.5.1.

Like the original mod's skills, Halley and Luna **permanently change the land**. Back up worlds you care about.

## SA-01 · Halley

Flip the cover and press the button. Your crosshair is marked in ice-blue and an approach corridor is projected across the land beyond it. A comet rises over the horizon with its tails stretched across the sky, hits the atmosphere as a fireball, touches down at the far end of the corridor, ploughs a frozen trench back toward you and detonates on the mark. It leaves an icy crater with the comet's glowing heart standing in it, and rays of frost thrown out over the land round it.

*   **The sky goes dark as it comes in**: stars, the band of the galaxy and an aurora come out, and the comet blazes across them. The fireball outshines the stars and the blast lights up the night. Then the air fills with ice: a pale haze with a halo, sun dogs and a light pillar.
*   Pieces break off the comet and burst in the sky, ice is thrown out of the crater on long arcs, glowing cracks race across the land, a wall of snow rides the shock front, and the comet heart shines a beam into the sky.
*   **Its own film** (skippable with the remote's cutscene key), with its title card, **SA-01 HALLEY**, streaking in like small comets.
*   Its own key (**O** by default, **G** on Fabric 26.3), menu card, sliders, cooldown, sounds, and two new blocks: the **Comet Heart** and the **Frozen Comet Trail**.
*   Everything in the trench and the crater is erased, you too if you stand there. Past the crater, the shock wave throws, hurts and freezes whatever it meets.

## SA-02 · Luna

Flip the cover and press the button: **EARTH SYSTEM SHUT DOWN**, and the sky falls to night. The moon, right where it hangs in your sky, shudders and cracks open along glowing molten seams, tears loose and falls onto your crosshair, faster and faster, burning through the air until it fills the sky.

*   **The impact**: a white flash, a shock ring, and a crater more than twice the moon's width, with the moon driven deep into it: burnt black and still glowing, veins of molten rock, a band of lava and a ragged rim of upturned crust. Fissures tear out across the land and lumps of moon and crust rain down far and wide.
*   **Its own film**, with its title card, **SA-02 LUNA**, cracking in and breaking away, and the impact in slow motion.
*   **Its own music**, cut so the drop lands the moment it hits (under your Music volume; **M** in the remote's menu switches it off). Music by [DOM\_MusicST](https://www.youtube.com/@DOM_MusicST) and [FarzanNB](https://www.youtube.com/@FarzanNB).
*   **Epic mode** (**G** in the remote's menu, **Z** on Fabric 26.3): the moon falls slower and more ominously, 24 seconds from the alarm to the impact instead of 16, for everyone watching, with a longer cut of its music.
*   **Keep the moon** (**L**): the moon model stays where it landed instead of crumbling, cooling down, until you switch it off. Made for filming.
*   **Light on the server**: nothing is loaded or generated for it. The crater and the moon are built where land is already loaded, on worker threads, so even huge strikes don't stall the server.
*   Works by day or night, but not where there is no moon, like the Nether. Its own key (**N** by default), sliders, cooldown, sounds and alarm.

### Moon cheese

The fallen moon is made of it.

*   **Moon cheese** and **holey moon cheese**: right-click a block to place it, right-click the air to eat it. It's as good as a golden carrot, and being cheese, it clears your effects like milk.
*   **Molten moon cheese**, one block at the moon's very centre: **500 hearts** and **Strength 255** for 10 minutes (Molten Might), and a full hunger bar.

## SA-00 · Stellar Convergence

A remote skill that fires every weapon you tick, all at once, timed so every impact lands on the same tick: Halley, Luna and The Shooting Star's Railgun, Gungnir and Seven Stars (Tesseract fires along with them). Gravitia, if ticked, comes last, once the others are done. Other addons' remote skills can be ticked too.

*   **Its panel**: select SA-00 in the remote's menu (**H**): **1-9** tick the weapons, **R** switches where they land (on the crosshair, or spread in a ring with room for each weapon's size), **X** the camera shake, **V** folds the panel.
*   **Its own film**: quick cuts to each weapon's moment, frozen into comic-style panels that shatter as everything hits.
*   **Master sliders**: each weapon fires with its own sliders, times SA-00's power and speed.
*   **Unlocked by Stellar Quartet** on NeoForge 1.21.1, Forge 1.21.1, Fabric 1.21.1 and Fabric 26.3: it fires only when you have [Stellar Quartet](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/the-shooting-star-stellar-quartet) by ikun520 installed, the addon that inspired it. On the other versions, which Stellar Quartet isn't made for, everyone gets it.
*   **No limits**: everything at once at high power can freeze a server. Aim far away.

## 🎚️ Sliders, like the original's

Halley and Luna have The Shooting Star's three sliders, under them in the remote's menu (**H**): **Power** 0.1× to 10×, **Damage** 2 to ∞ (∞ erases), **Speed** 0.3× to 3×. Server owners can cap them for every skill on the remote with `max_power`, `max_damage` and `max_speed` in `config/shooting_star_addition-common.toml`.

## The remote's menu

A small plate in the corner of the remote's menu (**H**) holds your own switches: **M** Luna's music, **G** Epic mode (**Z** on 26.3), **L** keep the moon, **F** impact frames (full, softer or off), **E** where a creative caster goes when standing where their own strike lands (straight up, or out to the crater's edge), **C** no cooldown.

## ⏱️ No cooldown (optional)

Off by default. Set `no_cooldown = true` in the `[stellar_remote]` section of `config/shooting_star_addition-common.toml` and creative players (survival too with `no_cooldown_survival = true`) can cast every skill on the remote again straight away, The Shooting Star's own included. Each player switches it with **C** in the menu. Be careful with this!

## Operator command

`/ssa strike <skill> [at <x y z>] [power] [damage|inf] [speed]` (operators) casts any remote skill as you, The Shooting Star's own too, aimed where you look or at a point, with no cooldown. **It ignores the server's slider limits**: values past the remote's own range warn you in chat, as they can freeze or crash a server.

## More

*   **Protected blocks**: the block tag `shooting_star_addition:unbreakable` (bedrock, barriers, command and structure blocks, end portals and gateways, reinforced deepslate) is never carved by Halley, Luna or Proxima; servers can add more with a data pack.
*   **Protected things**: the entity type tag `shooting_star_addition:never_removed` is never deleted by a strike (grave mods' corpses and Create's trains and contraptions by default); servers can add more.
*   **Radiation zones**: `/ssa zones [list|clear]` (operators) lists Proxima's radiation zones in your world or clears them.
*   **A key for Gravitia**, The Shooting Star's skill that has none: in the remote's menu and in Controls, unbound until you set it.
*   **Shader packs** (Iris): tested with Complementary Reimagined. Also tested with Sodium and ImmediatelyFast. **OptiFine** shaders aren't detected: set `shader_packs = "ALWAYS"` in `config/shooting_star_addition-client.toml`.

## Settings

*   `config/shooting_star_addition-common.toml` (the server's): both skills' sizes, blast reach and cooldowns, whether they carve the land, `no_cooldown`, and the slider limits.
*   `config/shooting_star_addition-client.toml` (each player's own): the dark sky, the extra effects, the world-lighting flashes (turn them off if flashing light bothers you), and the menu plate's switches.

## Will it work with future versions of The Shooting Star?

Not without an update of this addon. The Shooting Star has no official way for other mods to add skills, so this addon attaches itself to its internals, and those change names with every release.

*   **When The Shooting Star updates, wait for the next update of this addon.**
*   **It won't crash your game.** With a version it doesn't know, the game says so when it starts.
*   **Other mod loaders and Minecraft versions not listed above** are not supported yet.
